Paradigms are lines. Patterns are stations. Keystone stations are interchanges — learn one and several other lines become reachable without separate study. Frequency bars show how often a station shows up in real interview loops.

Modifier axes

These are not levels in the tree. They are toggles applied on top of any pattern, and they generate most of what looks like a brand-new problem. When a question feels unfamiliar, check whether it is a station you know with two or three of these flipped.

Pick a station to see its invariant, its recognition trigger, the lookalike that fools people, and the template.

Frequency is a judgement call from typical product-company loops, on a 1–5 scale: 5 near-certain, 4 very common, 3 common, 2 occasional, 1 rare or specialist.
